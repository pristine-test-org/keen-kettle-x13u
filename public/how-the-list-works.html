How the list works

Every item you add to Keen Kettle shows up in the shared list within a moment, and the five newest are always on the front page for everyone to see.

  • Add an item from any device.
  • It joins the top of the shared list.
  • Tick it off and it stays, marked as done.

The list keeps the five most recent items on the home page.
